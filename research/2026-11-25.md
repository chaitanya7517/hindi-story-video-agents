## Patterns
1. **Volume wins**: top channels have hundreds to 1,000+ videos -> our pipeline must make videos fast and repeatably.
2. **Most use 3D or bright 2D animation**; well-known tale types (Panchatantra, fairy tales, jungle animals) with a moral at the end.
3. **"Made for Kids" setting is required** for child audiences: comments and personalised ads are off (lower revenue per view).
4. **Thumbnails**: high contrast, big readable text, clear faces/emotion; test variations.
5. **Shorts** (<=60 s) can point viewers to the long video; in one case study Hindi Shorts grew reach less than other languages.

## Suggestions for our channel
- Keep the Dadi-ki-kahani frame as our signature (recognisable, warm, family).
- Mix famous tales (searchable) with our original Ganesh/village stories.
- Make a 30–60 s Short from each video's best moment.
- Our current look is simple 2D; the big channels are 3D — improving visuals is the biggest long-term upgrade.
