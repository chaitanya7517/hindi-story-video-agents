# Research report: what works for Hindi kids' story videos (27 Nov 2026)

Written by the Research agent. Goal: learn what successful Hindi kids' story, moral story and mythology cartoon channels do, and decide what **we** should change in video #2.

Our video #1: [गणेश जी और टूटा पुल](https://youtu.be/X7lxLx7SXas), 9 min 10 s, 2D canvas cartoon, Dadi tells a story to Pari and Monu.
Today it has **3 views, 1 like**, and the channel has 2 subscribers. That is normal for day 1 of a new channel, so we can't judge the video yet. This report compares us with channels that are already working.

---

## 1. How this research was done

- **YouTube Data API (real numbers, read-only).** I ran 14 searches: 7 Hindi/English queries, each sorted by view count, once for all time and once for videos published in the last 3 months (after 27 Aug 2026). That gave 309 videos from 209 channels, and I pulled views, likes, comments, length, publish date and subscriber count for each. Quota used was about 1,430 of 10,000 units.
  Queries: "हिंदी कहानी बच्चों के लिए", "moral stories in hindi cartoon", "ganesh ji ki kahani cartoon", "dadi maa ki kahaniyan", "panchatantra hindi cartoon", "bal ganesh story hindi", "gaon ki kahani cartoon".
- **Views per day** = total views ÷ days since upload. It shows what is working *now*, not just what is old and big.
- **Thumbnails.** I looked at 16 thumbnails from top videos and compared them with ours.
- **Comments.** I read the top comments on 8 big videos. Made-for-kids videos have comments switched off, so these come from family-audience videos.
- **Our own video.** I grabbed 8 frames from `projects/ganesh-dadi/output/draft.mp4` and checked its audio and its shot timeline.
- **Web.** I read YouTube help pages and news on kids-content rules, the AI "inauthentic content" policy, Shorts and money (links in Sources).

The search results were full of rhymes, craft Shorts and "Ghibli village ASMR". I removed those and kept **story / moral / mythology** videos.

### 2c. Key numbers in plain words

- **Small new channels can break out.** Among the top 40 rising long story videos, the **middle channel has only about 54,000 subscribers**. KATHA SAGAR got 34.5M views with only 7 videos, and LADDU GOPAL got 2.2M with 6. YouTube is pushing these videos to new viewers.
- **Long videos win.** Among the top 40 rising long story videos, the middle length is **about 20 minutes**. Among recent story videos over 3 minutes, the 25+ minute group did best (middle value about 55,000 views/day), then 15–25 minutes (about 38,000/day), then 8–15 minutes, where we are (about 26,000/day).
- **Shorts get huge view counts.** Recent story videos under 3 minutes had a middle value of about 120,000 views/day. A 30-second moral Short got 46M views on a 55K-subscriber channel.
- **Ganesh is a strong topic, but the hits show *Bal Ganesh as a living cartoon character*,** not as a statue. 5 of the top 7 rising mythology hits are Ganesh stories.
- **Only 35% of the top 40 rising story videos are "made for kids".** Most hits are aimed at families or adults ("watch while eating" is the most-liked comment type). See Downsides.
- **Title style:** 80% use the "|" separator, 38% use an emoji, and 20% use 😱/😭/😢. The hook in the title is a question or a surprise, like "फिर हुआ चमत्कार", "क्यों परेशान है…?" or "आधी रात को कौन…?".
- **Channels that upload a lot grow faster.** 90s Wala Gaon posts about 8 videos/week and Story Baatli about 19/week. The Bal Ganesh channel has 817 videos and Shemaroo is on "Part 36". Volume and series help.

## 3. What successful channels do (and how we compare)

| Area | What the hits do | What we did in video #1 |
|---|---|---|
| **Thumbnail** | One or two **big, close-up faces with strong emotion** (crying, shocked, angry, open mouth). A **clear conflict** is visible: Ganesh tied to a tree, a boy pointing a stick, a ghost at the window, Shiv with a trishul. Rich, detailed 3D/"Ghibli"-style art with warm light. Text is either **none** (about half) or **2–5 huge words** in white/yellow with a dark outline. Bright saturated colours, often a night-blue vs warm-orange contrast. | Many small, far-away figures. Everyone has the same neutral smile. Nothing is happening. Dark brown background. 6-word title + "दादी की कहानी" badge. The Ganesh murti is the biggest thing, but it is not a character with an emotion. |
| **Title** | The character name comes first, then a question or surprise ("बिल्ली क्यों परेशान है बाल गणेश जी से?", "…फिर हुआ चमत्कार 😱"), then "| Hindi Kahani | Moral Story". | "गणेश जी और टूटा पुल \| दादी की कहानी \| …": clear, but no question, surprise or emotion. |
| **First 10–30 s** | The story starts at once with action or a mystery. 90s/Nani videos start with a spooky or cosy scene plus a question. Mythology videos open on the most dramatic moment. | 25 s on **one static shot** with the title on screen while the kids squabble. The real problem (storm breaks the bridge) only comes at **2:12**. |
| **Length** | 13–30 min for long videos (middle value about 20 min). Big catalogues also post 30–60 min compilations. | 9 min. |
| **Art style** | Almost all rising channels use **AI-made 3D/Pixar-like or "Ghibli" images**: detailed villages, temples, food, firelight. Old giants (Shemaroo, JOJO, CVS) use real 3D animation. | Flat vector 2D, simple shapes, plain backgrounds. Clean and readable, but it looks like a cheap template next to these. |
| **Animation / motion** | AI-image channels mostly use **still images with slow camera moves (zoom/pan) and a new picture every 3–6 s**. Real 3D channels have full movement. | 39 shots in 9 min = **about 14 s per shot on average (longest 31 s)**, with little camera movement. |
| **Voices** | One narrator plus character voices, very expressive (gasps, crying, laughter). Commenters praise "pure Hindi" and **complain about Urdu words** ("Itni sari urdu kyu…") and Hinglish. | Cloned Hindi voices, decent and clear. Dadi narrator is a good fit. |
| **Music & sound effects** | Always background music, bhajan/flute themes for mythology, and sound effects (thunder, bells, crowd, footsteps). | **None. The audio between lines is total silence (-91 dB).** |
| **Characters** | A recurring cute hero (Bal Ganesh + Mushak, Chintu, Tuni the bird, Nani + grandkids). The hero kids (Golu/Meena) are new each story. |
| **Series** | "Part 36", "Episode 9", the same hero every video, playlists. | One-off video. |
| **Emotion & story type** | Mythology with a twist ("untold story"), devotion + miracle (चमत्कार), poor-but-honest village family, 90s nostalgia, gentle spooky stories with Dadi/Nani. | Village + Ganesh + teamwork moral: the right family of topics. |

**What viewers say in comments** (8 videos read):
- They love good AI visuals when they look rich and consistent: "Esko bolte hei AI ka real use" (258 likes), "रोंगटे भर गए" (159 likes).
- They notice mistakes. One commenter pointed out a boy's choti (hair tuft) appearing and disappearing between shots, and another wrote "Gemini logo was visible 😂". Character consistency matters.
- Devotional viewers comment "Jai Shree Ganesh / Har Har Mahadev" in large numbers. Mythology brings an adult devotional audience as well as kids.
- Parents: "This should reach all the kids" (187 likes). "Ye pahli kahani … jisme koi jalne wale nhi… sare gaon wale supportive" (95 likes): kind, warm stories are welcome.
- Many viewers are teens/adults: "khana khate samaye kaun dekhta hai" (1,961 likes), "Meri age 22 h…".
- They complain when the moral is missing ("Story me moral to diya hi nhi") or when the story changes scripture without explaining.

## 5. Honest downsides and limits

1. **These numbers show winners, not the average.** Searches sorted by views only show the top. For every KATHA SAGAR there are thousands of channels with 50 views. Expect slow growth for the first 10–20 videos.
2. **"Made for kids" costs money and features.** For made-for-kids videos, YouTube turns off comments, the notification bell, end screens, cards, the mini-player and personalised ads. Third-party estimates for kids RPM are very low (about $0.33–$1 per 1,000 views), and Hindi pays less than English. **65% of the rising story hits are *not* marked made for kids** because they target families/adults. But if our video is really made for children, the law (COPPA) and YouTube's rules say we **must** mark it made for kids. Don't mislabel it to get comments. A real choice is open: make "family" stories (Dadi + nostalgia + devotion) for all ages, or stay clearly kids-only.
3. **AI-content policy risk.** Since July 2025, and clarified on 16 July 2026, YouTube does not pay channels with mostly "inauthentic" content: generic/repetitive/template videos (including easy AI/CGI with little variation) and content made to upset or emotionally manipulate. The sobbing-orphan / "garib" thumbnails in our data are exactly this risk. Using AI is allowed, but each video needs real story work and variety. Realistic-looking AI must be disclosed; cartoon/animated AI usually doesn't need a label.
4. **Mythology is sensitive.** Commenters attack changes to scripture. For original Ganesh stories, Dadi should make clear it is *her* story inspired by Ganesh ji, not a scripture claim, and never show the gods in a disrespectful way.
5. **Better visuals cost time and money.** Our free canvas pipeline is cheap. AI images bring cost and consistency problems (other channels get caught with changing hair, logos, extra fingers). We need a consistency check in the Reviewer agent.
6. **Gaps in this research.** API search returns a limited set and may miss channels. Subscriber counts are rounded. I read only about 25 comments per video on 8 videos. Made-for-kids videos have no comments, so the true kid/parent voice is under-sampled. **We have no retention data yet.** After 1–2 weeks, open YouTube Studio → our video → Analytics → Audience retention to see exactly where people leave. That is the best evidence we'll ever have.
7. **Day 1.** Our video is a few hours old with 3 views, so none of this says video #1 failed. These are changes to make video #2 more clickable and more watchable.
